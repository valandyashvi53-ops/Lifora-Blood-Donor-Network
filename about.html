People first, always

Good coordination can make all the difference.

A donor may be ready. A hospital may need a specific blood group. A blood bank needs accurate records to screen, store, and issue every unit responsibly. Lifora models how those records can stay connected.

Our project demonstrates donor registration, blood-group stock visibility, request coordination, and traceable donation records. Real donations and stock are always confirmed by licensed professionals.

The principles behind the network

01 / PRIVACY

Protect donor details

Personal contact information belongs in authorised workflows, not a public donor list.

02 / TRACEABILITY

Track every unit

Donation, screening, component, expiry, and issue records connect through unique IDs.

03 / VERIFICATION

Confirm before action

Demo stock is illustrative. A registered blood bank verifies live inventory and eligibility.
